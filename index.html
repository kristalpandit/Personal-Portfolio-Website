I like work where I can start with a question, build something around it, and leave behind something useful.

Research

Incubate Nepal 2026 · Natural Language Processing

GPT Quantifiers

How much scientific writing appears to have been modified by AI?

When people discuss AI-written research, most existing studies focus on repositories such as arXiv and bioRxiv. We wanted to know whether the same pattern appeared in a different part of the research world.

Our project studied papers from open-access journals across five fields: Computer Science, Medicine, Education, Business, and History.

Using a statistical method developed in earlier research, we estimated how the prevalence of LLM-modified sentences changed from 2022 through 2024.

Before trusting the method on real papers, we tested it ourselves. We created mixtures containing known amounts of human and AI-rewritten text and asked whether the estimator could recover the correct percentage. In the range most relevant to our real data, its estimates were within roughly 0.9–1.6 percentage points of the true value.

Then we applied it to the journal data.

Words that leaned AI

delves · nuanced · pivotal · intricate · showcasing · interplay · realm · fostering

Words that leaned human

because · done · uses · get · lot · know · describe · useful

One thing I found particularly interesting was that AI-associated language was not limited to technical terminology: a small set of words appeared disproportionately often in text estimated to contain more LLM modification.

The project made me much more cautious about treating a statistical estimate as an answer by itself. A number is only useful if you understand what assumptions produced it.

Environmental Science · Project Lead

Groundwater Quality in Bharatpur

How safe is the groundwater that families around Bharatpur use every day?

I led a team of four students, working with two faculty advisers, to collect groundwater samples from 20 locations across Bharatpur Metropolitan City.

We tested each sample for pH, arsenic, coliform bacteria, hardness, chloride, iron, and nitrate, using our school laboratory and a professional laboratory for measurements requiring additional equipment.

The results surprised us.

Half of the samples tested positive for coliform bacteria. Thirteen of twenty exceeded our hardness threshold. One location contained 100 ppb of arsenic, ten times the WHO guideline value of 10 ppb.

At the same time, iron and nitrate remained within the relevant limits across all twenty samples.

The project taught me something I had not fully appreciated before: water can look completely normal while still containing something you cannot see.

We recommended repeat testing at the highest-risk locations, retesting coliform-positive wells, household treatment where necessary, and a larger study covering different seasons.

Community Work

Bal Sapana Foundation

In 2025, several friends and I became involved in creating Bal Sapana Foundation, a locally registered effort supporting children from low-income backgrounds.

Because I was under the required age, I was not one of the people who formally registered the organization. My involvement has instead been in its activities.

We have raised money for children in Chitwan and Kathmandu and visited orphanages to understand what support was actually needed rather than treating fundraising as an abstract exercise.

Balancing this work with school has sometimes limited how much we can do, but I have learned that community work does not always need to begin at a huge scale. It can begin with a few people deciding to consistently show up.
